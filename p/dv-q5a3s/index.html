§ 04

If Something Trips

Note I

A breaker has tripped

Find the lever in the lower position, switch off what is connected to that circuit, and lift the lever back up. If it trips again immediately, leave it off and call us. There is a fault on the circuit.

Note II

One phase is dead

Each phase has its own voltage & current relay. If one row of circuits is off and the others work, that phase's relay has cut it. It reconnects on its own once the voltage is back in range. If it keeps cutting out, call us.

Note III

Generator

The transfer switch (ATS) moves the house to the generator and back automatically. Do not touch the generator ATS control group on the top rail. If the generator does not take over during an outage, call us.

Note IV

Whole house off, nothing tripped

Check the Wi-Fi switch of the main contactor first: if it was switched off remotely, the whole house goes dark. If it is on, it is a grid-side outage and the generator should take over.

Note V

Monthly RCD test

Once a month, press the T button on each of the three RCDs (hall & kitchen, LED, pool). The lever should snap down. Lift it back up.

Note VI

Pool runs at the wrong time

The pool is switched by the timer relay on row L2. Its schedule is set with the MENU / ▲ / ▼ / OK buttons. If the display is blank, check the RELAY SUPPLY breaker (C10).
